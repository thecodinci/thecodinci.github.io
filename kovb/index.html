The Team Experience

Three levels, one program — every Panther volleyball athlete builds toward the same standard of competition and character.

Varsity

Varsity

The program's top competitive level, representing Klein Oak High School in district and postseason play.

View Team

Junior Varsity

Junior Varsity

The developmental step between Freshman and Varsity, building toward varsity-level competition.

View Team

Flex

Flex

A competitive level alongside Junior Varsity, giving more players match experience across the season.

View Team

Freshman

Freshman

The entry level for incoming 9th graders, focused on fundamentals and program culture.
