Codinci is a modern professional service provider and startup at the intersection of creativity and human-centered design that focuses on manufacturing, energy, and healthcare industries.

Mission

Building a better world through human-centered services and innovation.

The Name

Why “Codinci”?

Inspired by Leonardo da Vinci, the name Codinci fuses code with da Vinci — honoring creativity, intellect, and timeless innovation.

It represents the belief that human-centered service is an art, and innovation should be powerful and beautiful.
